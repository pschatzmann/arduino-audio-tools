Detailed Description

Tremolo AudioEffect: periodic change of the volume (amplitude modulation) between (100 - depth)% and 100%.

  • Waveform: Triangle (default) or Sine (smoother, like the "vibrato" of an electric piano)
  • Rate: as period in ms (setDuration()) or in Hz (setRate())
  • Auto pan: in a stereo AudioEffectStream each channel has its own clone of the effect; add the effect for the right channel with addEffect(1, effect) and setPhase(180) to move the sound between left and right.

The gain is calculated every 32 samples and interpolated linearly in between: per sample only one integer multiplication is needed (no float operation, no division), for the float and the fixed point configuration.
