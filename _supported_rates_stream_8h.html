class  SupportedRatesStream
 Resampling stream that is restricted to a fixed list of supported sample rates (e.g. the rates a DAC or codec chip can actually run at). On setAudioInfo() the closest supported rate is selected, the resampler is reconfigured to convert to it, and the corrected AudioInfo (with the supported sample rate) is forwarded to any subscribers/output.
