Detailed Description

Decoder for Ogg Container. Decodes a packet from an Ogg container. The Ogg begin segment contains the AudioInfo structure. You can subclass and overwrite the beginOfSegment() method to implement your own headers Dependency: https://github.com/pschatzmann/codec-opus.
