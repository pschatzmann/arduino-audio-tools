Detailed Description

MP3 Decoder using https://github.com/pschatzmann/codec-minimp3. This decoder does not provide any good results and it is not suited to decode any audio above 32000 on an ESP32. So the sample rate is limited by the MINIMP3_MAX_SAMPLE_RATE variable.
