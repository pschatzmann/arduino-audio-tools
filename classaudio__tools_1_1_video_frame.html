Detailed Description

Simple in-memory VideoOutput sink that assembles a single video frame of at most a fixed maximum size. Connect it to any write()/flush() producer (e.g. a decoder), then, once flush() has been called, read the assembled frame back via data() and available() (or register a setOnEnd() callback to be notified instead of polling).
