Detailed Description

Simple in-memory VideoOutput sink that assembles a single video frame of at most a fixed maximum size. Connect it to any beginFrame()/write()/endFrame() producer (e.g. a decoder), then, once endFrame() has been called, read the assembled frame back via data() and available().
