Detailed Description

template<typename T = float>
class audio_tools::audio_tools::BandPassFilter< T >

Second-order band-pass filter (BiQuad DF2). Passes frequencies near the center frequency and attenuates those further away. The bandwidth is controlled by the Q factor: higher Q produces a narrower passband. Coefficients are derived from the Audio EQ Cookbook.

Parameters
frequencycenter frequency in Hz
sampleRatesample rate in Hz
qquality factor (default 1.0; higher values narrow the passband)
