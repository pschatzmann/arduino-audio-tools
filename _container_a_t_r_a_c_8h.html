Detailed Description

Containers for ATRAC encoded audio: the decoders parse the header of the stream, configure a DecoderATRAC and forward the audio frames to it.

  • WAV (.at3, .at9) is handled by the WAVDecoder: register a DecoderATRAC for ATRAC3, ATRAC3PLUS and ATRAC9 with WAVDecoder::addDecoder()
  • ContainerATRACOMA: Sony OpenMG audio (.oma, .aa3) with ATRAC3 and ATRAC3+
  • ContainerATRACAEA: Sony MiniDisc Atrac1 (.aea)

The data can be written in chunks of any size. Encrypted OMA files are not supported.
